# 1:1 Google Photos Retrieval Interviews: Episode-Level Behavioral Evidence

> **Source Document:** `GOOGLE PHOTOS INTERVIEW (1).docx`  
> **Methodology:** In-Person 1:1 Task-Based Retrieval Protocol  
> **Scope:** 25 Distinct Retrieval Episodes across 6 Participants (Samidha, Maa, Durrani, Abhilasha, Rehan, Priya)  
> **Analytical Constraint:** Strict behavioral evidence analysis. No solutions, feature proposals, product changes, root causes, or conclusions are inferred. Participants' exact words are preserved, and observed behaviors are separated from participant interpretations.  
> **Evaluation Framework:** Six-Stage Failure Model:  
> `Memory` → `Search Expression` → `Interpretation` → `Retrieval` → `Ranking/Presentation` → `Navigation/Recognition` (or `None`)
